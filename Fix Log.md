The portfolio was already responsive and no major layout problems were found on mobile. Text, buttons, cards, and images displayed correctly without horizontal scrolling or overlapping elements.

## About Section Polish

### Before
The About section was functional and responsive, but visually plain. It consisted mainly of two paragraphs and four simple information cards.

### What I found
The section communicated the necessary information but did not strongly communicate my technical focus or personality as a developer.

### What I changed
I redesigned the About section with clearer visual hierarchy, a stronger introduction, technology tags, improved information cards, icons, spacing, borders, and subtle hover effects.

I also added responsive styling for tablet and mobile screen sizes.

### Result
The About section is now easier to scan, communicates my development interests more clearly, and has a more polished portfolio appearance while remaining responsive.
